MassRobotics is developing a marine robotics facility at the historic Boston Fish Pier, giving marine technology startups a dedicated space to build, launch, and test robots on Boston Harbor. Construction is underway through a partnership with the Massachusetts Port Authority (Massport), with the facility planned to open in early 2028 and programming starting throughout 2027.

The facility addresses a persistent obstacle for marine robotics companies: access to water for testing. According to MassRobotics, testing an underwater vehicle or ocean sensor typically requires renting boat time, borrowing a tank, or traveling to a remote site. The Fish Pier facility is designed to remove that barrier by letting companies design and fabricate in the MassRobotics machine shop, then walk their prototype to the pier and put it in the harbor the same day.

> Marine startups tell us that finding a place to test is one of their biggest hurdles, **said Tom Ryden, the executive director of MassRobotics.**

The Fish Pier facility will be outfitted with testing equipment for marine robotics, including a water tank, a float alongside the pier for direct water testing, and lifting and launch equipment for getting vehicles in and out of the harbor. MassRobotics currently supports more than 100 resident startups from across the globe, and the new site adds a marine dimension to its offerings while opening its doors wider to companies working in the blue economy.

Ryden framed the proximity as a strategic advantage: with the Fish Pier steps from MassRobotics headquarters, founders can build in the shop in the morning and test in the harbor by afternoon. He said the organization is grateful to Massport for the partnership and excited to help make Boston a global center for marine robotics. The facility is expected to tighten the loop between building and testing, enabling faster iteration for marine robotics developers.
