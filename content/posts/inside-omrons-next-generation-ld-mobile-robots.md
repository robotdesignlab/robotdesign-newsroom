Omron's next-generation LD mobile robots took center stage in Episode 264 of The Robot Report Podcast, where host Mike Oitzman interviewed Miguel Garcés Moreno, product marketing manager for Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) for mobile robots at OMRON Industrial Automation Europe. The discussion centers on how Omron is positioning its LD line of autonomous mobile robots (AMRs) for modern factory floors.

Garcés Moreno leads product strategy across autonomous mobile robots, industrial automation, and force- and power-limited or collaborative robots. He holds an industrial engineering degree and a Master's in Industry 4.0, with technical and market expertise spanning AI vision, motion control, and PLCs. His current focus is helping manufacturers transform factory floors with flexible, space-efficient, and fully automated intralogistics systems.
