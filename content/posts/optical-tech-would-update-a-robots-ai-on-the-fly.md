A new optical receiver could one day be used to update AI model parameters without energy-demanding electrical links. The technology projects light directly onto a chip to stream data using less energy, potentially enabling real-time updates for robots and other AI systems.

The approach addresses the energy bottleneck of traditional electrical data transmission, which is a significant concern for mobile robots that need to update their AI models on the fly. By using light instead of electricity, the system could reduce power consumption while maintaining high data throughput.

> A new optical receiver could one day be used to update AI model parameters without energy-demanding electrical links.

While the article does not provide specific experimental results or hardware details, the concept suggests a path toward more efficient on-the-fly AI updates for robotics applications. Further development and testing would be needed to assess practical performance.
