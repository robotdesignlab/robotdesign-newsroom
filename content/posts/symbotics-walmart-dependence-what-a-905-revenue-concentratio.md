Symbotic's latest quarterly filing identifies its largest customer, together with affiliates, as "Customer A" — named in the annual report as Walmart — which supplied 90.5% of revenue in the quarter ended June 27, 2026. For robotics founders weighing when to add a second customer, the disclosure offers a rare look at how deeply one anchor account can shape a company's finances and its contractual leverage.

Symbotic reported $720.8 million in revenue and $55 million in net income for that quarter, a scale few robotics companies have reached from a single demanding customer, and this profit follows earlier profits. The quarter also included $19.4 million of investment gains and negative non-GAAP free cash flow, so a single profitable period cannot settle whether leaning so heavily on Walmart has paid off.

> Few robotics companies have turned one demanding customer into a business this size, and **this profit follows earlier profits**.

The filings show Walmart did not rely on Symbotic's dependence alone for protection. The 2022 master automation agreement grants Walmart continuity rights, including the right to embed engineers with Symbotic and access technical material held in escrow under specified conditions. The 2025 agreement covering Walmart's store-level Accelerated Pickup and Delivery (APD) systems repeats those protections and, during the APD build-out, caps at a redacted number how many comparable APD systems Symbotic may sell to each outside customer per year. The filings do not show whether Walmart has ever exercised these rights, so they read more like preparation than a proven fallback.

When the APD deal closed in January 2025, Walmart committed $520 million of development funding, and Symbotic paid Walmart a $200 million base price for its robotics business in the same transaction. For founders, the 90.5% share says little about the cost of serving another account; the more useful questions are how many people an account pulls into the field, what the contract obliges each side to do when plans change, and how easily the anchor could get the same work done another way.
