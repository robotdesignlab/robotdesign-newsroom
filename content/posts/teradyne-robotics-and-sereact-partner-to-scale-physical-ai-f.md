Teradyne Robotics, the parent company of Universal Robots and Mobile Industrial Robots, has partnered with physical AI software developer Sereact to accelerate industrial AI solutions across retail, logistics, and manufacturing. The collaboration pairs Sereact's Cortex physical AI platform with Universal Robots' arms to automate unstructured tasks such as returns handling and piece picking — work that has traditionally resisted conventional automation.

The partnership arrives as retailers and logistics providers face growing pressure to process mixed goods, unknown items, and varied packaging without hard-coding every possible object. Sereact develops physical AI software that enables robots to grasp, identify, and handle objects they have never seen before — a capability the companies say is now moving beyond demonstration into live operations. The announcement was made at NVIDIA GTC Berlin 2026.

> The collaboration pairs Sereact's Cortex physical AI platform with Universal Robots' arms to automate unstructured tasks such as returns handling and piece picking — **work that has traditionally resisted conventional automation.**

At Zalando fulfillment centers in Germany and Poland, Sereact and Universal Robots now autonomously process returned fashion items. Together, the two companies have already shipped hundreds of UR robots in dual-arm systems to live customer sites across Europe. Jacob Pascual Pape, chief commercial officer of Teradyne Robotics, framed the fit around deployment scale: "Physical AI only matters when it performs in real-world operations, and that is where Universal Robots has always been at home." He noted that Universal Robots' platforms are open, connected, and proven through more than 100,000 industrial deployments, and that the joint solution handles mixed goods, unknown items, and varied packaging. "This has moved well beyond the demo or pilot stage with the technology now usefully deployed at real customer sites," he added.

To serve growing demand and bring the joint solution to North America, Sereact opened a U.S. headquarters in Charlestown, Massachusetts, this summer. The companies said their combined offering can help a variety of critical logistics and manufacturing applications, and that the partnership is intended to scale these AI-powered solutions quickly across applications, industries, and markets.
