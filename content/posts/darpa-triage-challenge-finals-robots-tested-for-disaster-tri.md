DARPA's latest grand challenge, the DARPA Triage Challenge, has reached its finals, where teams demonstrated whether robots could save lives in emergency response scenarios. The event is aimed at proving that robotic systems can play a meaningful role in disaster triage, where rapid assessment of victims is critical.

BACKGROUND: The challenge is described as the defense agency's latest grand challenge, built around the premise that robots could save lives in emergencies. In the finals scenario, a woman roleplaying a disaster victim called for help while a Spot robot from the University of Maryland's RoboScout team surveyed its surroundings. The setup places robots directly into triage situations alongside simulated casualties rather than controlled laboratory conditions.

KEY DETAILS: The source identifies the University of Maryland's RoboScout team as a participant in the finals, operating a Spot robot during the scenario.
