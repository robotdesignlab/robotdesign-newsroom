Agility Robotics and GXO Logistics are expanding their partnership to deploy Agility's Digit humanoid robot at GXO sites in North Carolina and Tennessee. The move extends a collaboration that began in 2023, when GXO became the first customer to put Digit to work in live operations under a commercial agreement.

"GXO was the first customer to put Digit to work in live operations under a commercial agreement, and expanding to additional sites is the clearest signal that the value is real," said Peggy Johnson, CEO of Agility. She framed the expansion as evidence that customers are moving past feasibility questions toward decisions about where humanoids can be scaled.

> "GXO was the first customer to put Digit to work in live operations under a commercial agreement, and **expanding to additional sites is the clearest signal that the value is real**," said Peggy Johnson, CEO of Agility.

Founded in 2015, Agility Robotics said it has accumulated more than 65,000 hours of real-world operation while working to industry safety standards. The Salem, Ore.-based company recently received U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission approval for its planned merger with special-purpose acquisition company Churchill Capital Corp. XI as it prepares to go public. Digit earned Agility the inaugural RBR50 Robot of the Year award in 2024 for being among the first humanoids to enter commercial trials, and received 2026 honors for moving more than 100,000 totes for GXO last year.

GXO Logistics, spun out of XPO Inc. in 2021, describes itself as "the world's largest pure-play contract logistics provider." The third-party logistics provider has more than 150,000 employees across over 1,000 facilities totaling more than 200 million sq. ft. (18.5 million sq. m). The Greenwich, Conn.-based company last month partnered with Exotec to automate fulfillment for Guess in the Netherlands.

The expansion comes amid a busy period for Agility: this month the company chose Nyobolt for fast charging, expanded its partnership with NVIDIA, and said it plans to deploy Digit at an AGCO factory for agricultural equipment in 2027. Johnson said years of performing real work in GXO's operations have shaped how Agility builds, deploys and supports Digit, and that the company is focused on disciplined execution as the partnership grows.
